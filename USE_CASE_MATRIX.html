MedCare Use Case Matrix

A functional overview of the MedCare Patient Record and Care Coordination System workflows and their actors.

System MedCare Patient Record and Care Coordination System
Scope Authentication, patient care, appointments, prescriptions, inventory, and care coordination
  • Patient Access
  • Doctor Workflows
  • Staff Workflows
  • Care Coordination
  • Patient Appointments

Authentication and Patient Access

UC-01: Sign In

Primary ActorDoctor, Staff, or Patient
TriggerA user opens the sign-in page and submits their username and password.
Basic Flow
  1. The user enters credentials.
  2. The system validates the credentials.
  3. The system starts an authenticated session and opens the dashboard for the user's role.
Post ConditionThe user is authenticated and can access pages permitted for their role.
Secondary ActorAuthentication and database services

UC-02: Sign Out

Primary ActorAuthenticated user
TriggerThe user selects the sign-out action.
Basic Flow
  1. The system ends the user's session.
  2. The system redirects the user to the sign-in page.
Post ConditionThe session is no longer authenticated.
Secondary ActorAuthentication service

UC-03: Register Patient Account

Primary ActorNew patient
TriggerThe patient submits the patient-account registration form.
Basic Flow
  1. The patient enters account credentials and required personal and contact information.
  2. The system validates the submitted information.
  3. The system creates the patient account and associated patient record.
  4. The patient is signed in and redirected to the patient dashboard.
Post ConditionA patient account and patient record are available to the patient, subject to successful validation and account linkage.
Secondary ActorAuthentication and database services

UC-04: View Personal Health Information

Primary ActorPatient
TriggerThe patient opens their health record, treatments, or prescriptions.
Basic Flow
  1. The system identifies the patient associated with the signed-in account.
  2. The system retrieves and displays that patient's permitted information.
  3. The patient selects a treatment or prescription to view its details, when available.
Post ConditionThe patient has viewed their own health information; no other patient's information is disclosed.
Secondary ActorDoctor or Staff, as the source of clinical records

UC-05: Access Health Education

Primary ActorPatient
TriggerThe patient opens the health education library or selects a resource.
Basic Flow
  1. The system displays available health education resources.
  2. The patient selects a resource.
  3. The system displays the resource content.
Post ConditionThe selected health education resource is displayed to the patient.
Secondary ActorStaff, as the resource manager

Doctor Workflows

UC-06: Manage Patient Record

Primary ActorDoctor
TriggerThe doctor searches for a patient or starts a patient registration.
Basic Flow
  1. The doctor searches or opens the patient list.
  2. The doctor views a patient's record or enters information for a new patient.
  3. The system validates and saves submitted patient information.
Post ConditionThe patient record is available for authorized care workflows.
Secondary ActorPatient

UC-07: Schedule or Manage Appointment

Primary ActorDoctor
TriggerThe doctor opens the appointment calendar or starts an appointment action.
Basic Flow
  1. The doctor selects a patient, date, time, duration, and appointment details.
  2. The system checks for an overlapping appointment for that doctor.
  3. If the time is available, the system saves the appointment.
  4. The doctor can view, edit, reschedule, or update its status from the calendar.
Post ConditionThe appointment is recorded or updated; overlapping appointments for the same doctor are rejected.
Secondary ActorPatient

UC-08: Record Consultation, Diagnosis, and Treatment

Primary ActorDoctor
TriggerThe doctor starts a consultation for a patient.
Basic Flow
  1. The doctor selects the patient.
  2. The doctor records consultation details, diagnosis, and treatment information.
  3. The system validates and saves the clinical record.
  4. The record becomes part of the patient's consultation history.
Post ConditionThe consultation and related clinical information are saved to the patient's record.
Secondary ActorPatient

UC-09: Create Prescription

Primary ActorDoctor
TriggerThe doctor chooses to prescribe medication for a patient.
Basic Flow
  1. The doctor selects the patient and enters prescription details and medication items.
  2. The system checks medication availability and expiration status.
  3. The system saves the prescription when the submitted items pass validation.
Post ConditionA prescription is recorded for the patient and can be reviewed by authorized users.
Secondary ActorStaff, for prescription dispensing

UC-10: Set Patient Priority

Primary ActorDoctor
TriggerThe doctor identifies a need to classify or update a patient's care priority.
Basic Flow
  1. The doctor opens the patient's record.
  2. The doctor selects a priority level.
  3. The system saves the updated priority.
Post ConditionThe patient's priority classification is updated.
Secondary ActorStaff

Staff Workflows

UC-11: Manage Inventory and Stock

Primary ActorStaff
TriggerStaff adds or edits an inventory item, records stock received or removed, or reviews stock alerts.
Basic Flow
  1. Staff selects an inventory action.
  2. Staff enters the item or stock transaction details.
  3. The system validates the quantity and item information.
  4. The system saves the change and records the inventory transaction.
  5. Staff reviews low-stock or expiration alerts as needed.
Post ConditionInventory quantities and transaction history reflect the accepted change; invalid negative stock is prevented.
Secondary ActorDoctor, when checking medication availability

UC-12: Dispense Prescription

Primary ActorStaff
TriggerStaff opens an eligible prescription for dispensing.
Basic Flow
  1. Staff reviews the prescription and its medication items.
  2. The system checks available stock and medication expiration status.
  3. Staff confirms dispensing.
  4. The system records the dispense action, deducts stock, and logs the inventory transaction.
Post ConditionThe prescription status and inventory reflect the dispensing transaction; expired or insufficient stock is not dispensed.
Secondary ActorDoctor, as the prescription author; Patient, as recipient

UC-13: Maintain Health Education Resources

Primary ActorStaff
TriggerStaff creates, edits, publishes, or views a health education resource.
Basic Flow
  1. Staff opens health education management.
  2. Staff enters or updates resource information.
  3. The system validates and saves the resource.
  4. Published resources are made available in the patient library.
Post ConditionThe resource is saved and, when published, available to patients.
Secondary ActorPatient

Shared Care Coordination

UC-14: Record Treatment Progress

Primary ActorDoctor or Staff
TriggerAn authorized care team member records an update for a patient's treatment.
Basic Flow
  1. The user selects a patient and associated treatment.
  2. The user enters the progress update.
  3. The system validates and saves the update in the treatment history.
Post ConditionThe progress update is linked to the selected treatment and can be reviewed by authorized users.
Secondary ActorPatient

UC-15: Send and Review Team Messages

Primary ActorDoctor or Staff
TriggerA care team member opens team messages to send a message or review received messages.
Basic Flow
  1. The sender selects a recipient and enters the message, optionally linking it to a patient.
  2. The system validates and saves the message.
  3. The recipient reviews the message and can mark it as read.
Post ConditionThe message is available to its recipient with its read status recorded.
Secondary ActorThe selected healthcare team recipient; Patient, when the message is linked to a patient record

Patient Appointment Workflows

UC-16: Request or Cancel Own Appointment

Primary ActorPatient
TriggerThe patient opens My Appointments to request an appointment or cancel an existing appointment.
Basic Flow
  1. To request an appointment, the patient selects a doctor, date, time, appointment type, and reason, then submits the request.
  2. The system validates and records the appointment request for the selected doctor.
  3. To cancel, the patient selects one of their own eligible appointments and confirms cancellation.
  4. The system verifies ownership and eligibility before updating the appointment status.
Post ConditionThe appointment request is recorded or the patient's eligible appointment is cancelled; the patient cannot cancel another patient's appointment.
Secondary ActorDoctor
Note: Access to patient and clinical information depends on the user's authenticated role and the system's authorization checks. This matrix describes the application's workflows at a functional level and does not specify internal database or interface implementation details.
